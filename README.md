## NFL Survivor Pool

A weekly NFL pick 'em pool. Every week you pick one team — get it right and
it counts as a win, get it wrong and it counts as a loss, but either way
you're still in it. There's no elimination, just a season-long win-loss
record and bragging rights.

### How it works

- Sign up with your email to join the pool.
- Each week, pick any team playing that week. You can change your mind as
  many times as you want, right up until that week's first game kicks off.
- Once you pick a team, you can't pick that same team again for the rest of
  the season — so choose wisely.
- The next week opens up once every game from the current week has
  finished. Tabs across the top let you browse any week, past or upcoming.
- If you miss the deadline and don't pick in time, you'll automatically be
  assigned a random team you haven't used yet, so you're never left out.
- Scores and results update automatically as games finish — no need to
  check ESPN yourself. Once your pick's game ends, it turns green if you
  won or red if you lost.
- Standings are sorted by record (most wins, then fewest losses), so you
  can always see where you stand against everyone else in the pool.
- `/rules` has the full rundown of how the pool works if you ever need a
  refresher.
